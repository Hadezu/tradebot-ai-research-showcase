# One system, several connected responsibilities

This walkthrough describes research paths inspected in the current local research branch and its uncommitted changes on 20 October 2026. It is a conceptual explanation, not an exported run or acceptance of every mode.

## Follow one research decision

1. **Prepare available history.** Observations have a time boundary. A later result cannot become an input to an earlier decision.
2. **Build alternatives.** Native strategies produce signals or explicit non-signal/error states. An optional research mode explores directional actions when native strategies are quiet. Doing nothing stays available.
3. **Check the candidate.** Features, target meaning, model identity and execution assumptions must agree. A forecast is not authorization.
4. **Allocate a shared account.** A historical executor handles competing requests, occupied capital, costs and positions. Adding independent opportunity outcomes would not produce a valid portfolio return.
5. **Continue the account.** Subsequent windows retain positions and financial state. Existing positions retain their entry context when selection rules change. Unknown execution cannot be resolved by inventing a favourable fill or resetting the account.
6. **Wait for the outcome.** Results become eligible for learning only after their availability boundary and required checks. Simulated evidence is not exchange execution evidence.
7. **Train and compare.** Experimental entry and management candidates are evaluated under compatible conditions. Rejection, retaining an eligible incumbent and no action are valid outcomes. Evaluation does not automatically activate a model.
8. **Preserve the explanation.** Artifact identities, completion records and checkpoints support investigation and recovery. An uncertain save can require manual review.

Changing a management policy can change the outcome learned at entry. Changing that outcome definition can invalidate previously trained artifacts. Maintaining these connections is a substantial part of the engineering.

## Three different uses of AI

| Use | Role | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT and Codex during development | Analyse problems, implement changes and run checks under the owner's direction | Reports require review; this is not unaided manual engineering. |
| Numerical models inside research | Estimate action outcomes and investigate entry or management choices | Durable economic advantage is unproven. |
| Advisory analysis components | Support research interpretation and summaries | Their presence does not establish effective autonomous decision-making. |

Deterministic checks govern compatibility, accounting and eligibility. This showcase does not demonstrate an LLM controlling a live account.

## Why the connections matter outside trading

| TradeBot problem | Similar business software problem |
|---|---|
| Old labels no longer describe the current executor | Reusing data after business rules change |
| Output saved before completion is recorded | Recovering an interrupted batch job without duplicate work |
| Successful training without evaluation | An artifact reaching approval without required evidence |
| Decisions compete for limited capital | Scheduling tasks against shared capacity and reservations |
| Unknown outcomes must stay explicit | Distinguishing missing data from a negative result |

These are transferable design problems, not claims of previous paid client delivery.

## Evidence boundaries

Inspected code and tests establish the existence of these responsibilities. The saved acceptance supports specific behaviors in the [case studies](engineering-case-studies.md), not universal correctness or every uncommitted change.

Learned protection/reduction selection remains incomplete, despite tested target and financial interfaces. Previously explored history is exploratory, not an independent holdout. Profitability, unattended live operation and production readiness are not established.
